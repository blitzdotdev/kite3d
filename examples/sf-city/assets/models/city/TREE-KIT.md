# SF tree geometry iteration

Original static generator `tree-geometry.mjs` replaces disconnected cylinders and uniform ellipsoid cards with a flared curved trunk, six tapered scaffold limbs, eighteen secondary branches, thirty-six terminal twigs and attached foliage sprays. Three deterministic growth habits remain approximations, not surveyed species reconstruction. Existing inventory IDs, mapped positions and height overrides remain the placement source.

Each template has two meshes: 1,432 wood triangles plus 432 leaf triangles, 1,864 total. The template is normalized to ten metres and the existing placement scale supplies height. Crown-oriented normals and restrained vertex color darkening improve volume without custom shaders or continuous wind. Colors are ordinary glTF COLOR_0; the shared leaf atlas is original sRGB color, not shader data. Independent geometry inspection verifies outward winding, positive triangle/vertex normal agreement and finite attributes. An initial 108-card render was rejected because the crowns were too sparse; the current 216-card version uses broader original leaf clusters and larger sprays. The corrected park and church views were inspected in the pinned Kite viewer: connected crowns replace the rejected sparse render and no rectangular alpha-card backgrounds were visible. The crowns remain stylized, with narrow park silhouettes rather than reconstructed species. Final full-frame performance is recorded separately in validation/runtime-performance.json; this asset change is not a claim of photographic realism or citywide scalability.

No source code, atlas pixels, TSL bridge or mixed-license assets were copied. The NYC atlas encodes shader data and is not a suitable conventional albedo texture. WebGI's custom license is not assumed to cover unrestricted library redistribution. This is an original implementation of the geometric concepts using the pinned Three.js API.

Opt-in `node assets/models/city/build-tree-lods.mjs` now writes offline mid/far candidates at 876/552 triangles per template, compared with 1,864 near. It simplifies internal wood while preserving every foliage attribute and index byte for crown coverage. Manual checks verify all near bytes remain unchanged and all levels have finite attributes. These candidates are unregistered and excluded from the validated viewer ZIP. An isolated pinned-viewer comparison was rendered and inspected: crowns retain their outline while internal wood becomes simpler; zero browser errors. See validation/tree-lod-comparison.png. Runtime switch transitions and distance-dependent alpha behavior are not yet tested. Runtime distance switching, species-specific branching, alpha-coverage-preserving mipmaps and temporal wind remain future work. The current three templates are not a citywide performance solution by themselves; spatial runtime batching, material reuse and streaming are separate work. In particular, the PR12 G-buffer warning requires checking leaf cutouts in color/depth/shadows on our older renderer rather than importing its shader patch blindly.
