North Beach / Washington Square - Kite3D viewer demo

Static demo: serve index.html over HTTP(S), preserving all relative paths. No editor, login or API key is required. Do not open index.html directly with file://.

Map data: City and County of San Francisco / DataSF, PDDL. Building footprints, street lines and tree inventory are data-derived; facades, materials, tree shapes and much street detail are inferred. Ground interpolates older footprint elevations, not LiDAR.
Park layout: original geometry approximately traced from SF Rec & Park July 25 2018 concept plan, page 6, not a surveyed as-built. https://sfrecpark.org/DocumentCenter/View/1878/Community-Meeting-07-25-2018-PDF
Church silhouette: original approximate model informed by published dimensions and inspected photo. https://www.salesiansspp.org/our-history . Reference photos and plan pixels are not distributed.

Adapted scale/orientation, externalized textures and static hierarchy. This is an approximate substitute vehicle, not the photographed car. No endorsement implied.

Generated architecture, textures, park furniture and foliage are original procedural assets. Runtime software license notices are retained in bundled JavaScript and included license files.
